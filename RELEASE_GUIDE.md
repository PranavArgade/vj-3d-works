# VJ-3D-WORKS Android Billing App — Release & Installation Guide

## 📱 About VJ-3D-WORKS Billing App
**VJ-3D-WORKS** is a standalone, offline-first commercial Android application designed specifically for 3D printing and rapid fabrication businesses. It empowers you to generate, customize, print, and track professional invoices and quotations without requiring an active internet connection or ongoing subscriptions.

---

## 🔒 100% Permanent & Offline Guarantee
- **No Expiration Date**: The application never expires (no 7-day, 30-day, or 1-year locks).
- **No License Checks**: Fully standalone with zero phone-home or activation requirements.
- **Offline Operations**: All calculations, customer records, pricing configurations, and PDF generation work completely offline.
- **Persistent Local Data**: Company settings, invoice history, and material rates remain safely stored on your device.

---

## 📥 How to Install the APK on Android Devices

1. **Transfer the APK**:
   - Transfer `VJ-3D-WORKS.apk` to your phone via USB cable, Google Drive, WhatsApp, or by downloading it directly from your GitHub Release link.
2. **Enable Unknown Sources (if prompted)**:
   - On modern Android (Android 8 to Android 15), tap on the APK file in your Files / Downloads app.
   - If prompted with *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*, tap **Settings** and toggle **Allow from this source**.
3. **Install**:
   - Tap **Install** and wait 5 seconds.
   - Tap **Open** to launch VJ-3D-WORKS!

---

## 🖨️ How to Save Invoices as PDF & Print on Android

1. Create or edit an invoice in the app.
2. Tap the **🖨️ Print / Save as PDF** button at the bottom of the invoice builder.
3. Android's native Print Spooler dialog will open:
   - At the top dropdown, select **Save as PDF** (or select a connected Wi-Fi/Bluetooth printer).
   - Tap the yellow/blue **PDF Download Icon** to save the invoice directly to your phone's **Downloads** folder.
4. Tap **📤 Share Invoice** to share the invoice summary and details via WhatsApp, Email, or Messaging apps.

## 🌐 Publishing to GitHub Releases (Permanent Download URL)

To give your clients a single, unchanging download URL:
1. Create a repository on GitHub (e.g., `https://github.com/your-username/vj-3d-works-app`).
2. Go to **Releases → Draft a new release**.
3. Set Tag: `v1.0.0`
4. Attach `VJ-3D-WORKS.apk`.
5. Click **Publish release**.
6. The permanent direct download link will be:
   ```
   https://github.com/<your-username>/vj-3d-works-app/releases/latest/download/VJ-3D-WORKS.apk
   ```
   *(This URL automatically redirects to the newest APK whenever you upload an update!)*
